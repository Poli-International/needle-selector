## 🎯 Overview

The Tattoo Needle Selector is an expert tool designed to take the guesswork out of needle selection. By choosing your desired technique (lining, shading, packing) and style, the tool recommends the optimal needle type, taper, and configuration.

**Key Use Cases:**

- Choosing between round liners, shaders, magnums, and flats
- Understanding the impact of needle tapers on skin trauma
- Optimizing needle selection for specific styles like Realism or Trad

**Target Audience:**

- Apprentice tattoo artists
- Professionals exploring new techniques

---

## ✨ Features

- ✅ **Smart Recommendations** - Configuration suggestions based on technique.
- ✅ **Needle Type Guide** - Detailed information on RL, RS, RM, and M1 configurations.
- ✅ **Taper Explanation** - Understand the difference between Short, Long, and Super Long tapers.
- ✅ **Responsive Design** - Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- ✅ **Free & Open Source** - MIT licensed.
